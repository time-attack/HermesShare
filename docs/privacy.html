HermesShare Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026

The short version

HermesShare collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no servers operated by us.

What the app does with data

HermesShare renders cards from JSON documents that arrive inside iMessage conversations. Message content is handled entirely by Apple's Messages app and stays within your conversations; HermesShare only reads the card payload of a message you tap, on your device, to draw it.

Local network (Agent Dashboard)

The optional agent dashboard connects directly to a server you run on your own local network (the Hermes agent harness). Status updates and preview images stream from your machine to your phone over your LAN. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party — we have no servers.

Data storage

Pairing details for your own harness server (its address and token) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/time-attack/HermesShare.

HermesShare is open source. This policy covers the App Store distribution of the app.
